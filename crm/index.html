Apple Wallet Passes and Google Wallet Passes with any CRM

Integration of Passquare service and any CRM offers opportunities for automating and managing your loyalty program:

Automatic issuance of digital loyalty cards

Your current and potential customers can easily obtain digital loyalty cards without requiring direct staff involvement. Cards can be easily added to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet applications.

Automatic POS synchronization

All issued cards appear in your CRM system. The cards display an identifier in the form of either the card number or the phone number. Synchronization occurs using the phone number. If a customer with that phone number does not already exist in your CRM, a new customer will be created. If the customer already exists, the system will link the new card with the existing customer, ensuring no duplicates are created. These cards are ready for customers to use.

Real-time information updates

Digital cards display up-to-date information about your company and customer privileges. We provide live-updating information to ensure your customers are informed of the latest news and offers.

Bonus tracking and notification

When a customer makes a purchase, their bonus balance is automatically updated, and bonus information is displayed on their loyalty card. Customers also receive notifications about their balance and the latest offers from your company.

Passquare and any CRM integration allows you to easily manage your loyalty program and ensure high levels of customer satisfaction with your services and products.

After issuing cards to your customers, you can integrate referral mechanics to organically grow your customer base. Next, you can configure automatic segments based on your customers' purchasing activities. For each segment, you can set up special promotions and triggers to manage customer activity effectively.
