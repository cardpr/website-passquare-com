Apple Wallet Passes and Google Wallet Passes with any CRM

Connect Passquare to any CRM to automate and run your loyalty program.

Issue digital loyalty cards automatically

Current and new customers can get a digital loyalty card without staff help. Cards add to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet in a few taps.

Automatic POS synchronization

Issued cards appear in your CRM with a card number or phone number as the ID. Sync uses the phone number: if the customer is new, Passquare creates them; if they already exist, the card is linked and duplicates are avoided. Cards are ready to use right away.

Real-time information updates

Digital cards show current company information and customer benefits, updated live so customers see the latest news and offers.

Bonus tracking and notification

After a purchase, the points balance updates on the loyalty card. Customers also get a notification about their balance and your latest offers.

With Passquare and your CRM, you can run the loyalty program from one place.

After cards are issued, add referrals to grow the customer base, then set up segments from purchase history. Give each segment its own offers and triggers.
