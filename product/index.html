Product features.

Issue and manage cards automatically

The customer fills in a short signup form and gets a wallet card in real time. Their details appear in your CRM at once. You can get rid of paper forms, plastic cards, and manual data entry. UTM support for analytics and CAPTCHA to block bots.

Live card layout

Build any card layout and show the data you need from your CRM, for example the customer’s current balance. The design matches your brand, and you can update it as often as you like.

Send push notifications

Tell customers about promotions, special offers, and updates. Send notifications from the Passquare dashboard, automatically from CRM data, by location, or via iBeacons.

Balance updates

Customers see balance changes and other CRM data on the wallet pass itself, with a lock-screen push notification.
