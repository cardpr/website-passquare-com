Product features.

Fully automatic issuance and management of cards

The client fills the online application form and get a wallet card in real time. The customer information immediately appears in your CRM system. Now you can rid of paper questionnaires, plastic cards and manual data transfer to the CRM system. UTM support for analytics and CAPTCHA integration to block automated requests from bots.

Wallet card live structure

Create any structure of the card and display on it the necessary data from the CRM-system, for example, the actual balance of the client. The design of the card is fully consistent with your corporate identity and you can update it as often as you like.

Send push messages

Let your customers know about promotions, special offers and your latest updates. Send messages manually from the Passquare dashboard, automatically based on CRM data, by location, or via iBeacons.

Balance update

Your customers receive up-to-date data on changes in balance and other information from the CRM system. The data changes on the wallet pass itself and is displayed in a pop-up push message.

Free start

Try Passquare — it’s free & easy.

You will be able to create an account, set up the card issuance form, set up the appearance of your cards, issue several wallet cards and send push messages to them.
