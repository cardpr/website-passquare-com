Loyverse integration

To set up the integration, you need:

  1. A loyalty program configured in Loyverse.
  2. An access token from your Loyverse account.
  3. The points earning percentage from Loyverse loyalty settings.

In Passquare settings, set the starting wallet card number. This number must be greater than the numbers of cards already created in the CRM.

Go to Integration. If a demo period is active, end it. Click the CRM system field and select Loyverse from the drop-down list.

  • In Server, enter `https://api.loyverse.com/v1.0`
  • In Password, enter the token from https://r.loyverse.com/dashboard/#/integrations/tokens
  • In Wallet ID, enter the points earning percentage from https://r.loyverse.com/dashboard/#/settings/loyalty

Click Save and turn the integration on. If you already have issued cards in your Passquare account, go to Customers and, in the lower-right corner, click Write to CRM.

Integration details

  • Bonus accrual and redemption are configured and processed in your Loyverse system.
  • After a card is issued, the customer and card usually appear in Loyverse within a few minutes to a few hours. The maximum delay is 24 hours. Timing depends on system load and the update queue.
  • When the bonus balance changes in Loyverse, the information appears in Passquare and on the customer card after the card is refreshed in the Passquare account or on the customer’s phone.
