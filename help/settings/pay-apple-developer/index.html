Pay for the Apple Developer Program

  • Make sure the iPhone or iPad you will use to renew the subscription is signed in with the same Apple ID that is used in the Apple Developer account. If needed, sign in on the device with that Apple ID.
  • In the device settings, add a payment method using this guide: https://support.apple.com/HT201266. A bank card is the usual option.
  • Find the Apple Developer Program subscription in the list of subscriptions in the App Store (this may differ depending on the iOS version) and make sure auto-renewal is turned off for this subscription. Turn auto-renewal off if needed.
  • Renew the subscription manually using the payment method you added.
  • Issue a new certificate and replace it in your Passquare account: Get and renew a Pass Type ID certificate
  • Go to Card templates in your account, select your template, and click Update all cards. Updating issued cards can take up to 24 hours.
