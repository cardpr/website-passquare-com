Apple Wallet Passes and Google Wallet Passes with Square POS

The integration of Square and Passquare services provides an excellent way to automate and manage your loyalty program:

Seamless distribution of digital loyalty cards

Customers, both existing and prospective, can effortlessly acquire digital loyalty cards without needing direct assistance from your staff. These cards can be conveniently added to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet applications.

Automatic POS synchronization

Every issued card is automatically added to your Square account. These cards are identified by either a card number or a phone number, making them immediately ready for customer use.

Real-time information updates

Digital cards show the most current information about your company and customer benefits. We ensure that information is constantly updated so your customers are always aware of the latest news and promotions.

Bonus tracking and notification

When a customer makes a purchase, their bonus balance is instantly updated and displayed on their loyalty card. Customers also receive notifications about their balance and the latest offers from your company.

Square and Passquare integration allows you to easily manage your loyalty program and ensure high levels of customer satisfaction with your services and products.

After issuing cards to your customers, you can incorporate referral mechanics to organically grow your customer base. You can then configure automatic segments based on your customers' purchasing activities. For each segment, you can set up special promotions and triggers to effectively manage customer activity.
