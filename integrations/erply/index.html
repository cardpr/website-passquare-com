Apple Wallet Passes and Google Wallet Passes with ERPLY

Connect ERPLY and Passquare to automate and run your loyalty program.

Issue digital loyalty cards automatically: current and new customers can get a card without staff help and add it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet in a few taps.

Automatic POS sync: issued cards appear in your ERPLY account with a card number or phone number as the ID. Cards are ready to use right away.

Live updates: digital cards show current company information and customer benefits, so customers see the latest news and offers.

Points tracking: after a purchase, the points balance updates on the loyalty card. Customers also get a notification about their balance and your latest offers.

With ERPLY and Passquare, you can run the loyalty program from one place.

After cards are issued, add referrals to grow the customer base, then set up segments from purchase history. Give each segment its own offers and triggers.
