Loyalty Wallet Pass

Analogue of a plastic card that is stored in your customer’s phone and, unlike a plastic card, provides a business the ability to update data on the card in real time, as well as engage in promotions and keep in touch with customers.

Segmentation

02

RFM-analysis

Analysis of client base by prescription, frequency, amount and composition of client checks. Allows to automatically segment the database and assign a unique design of digital cards, special promotions and sending push messages to each segment.

Contactless

Contactless NFC loyalty cards.

NFC technology allows you to accept wallet cards faster, more reliable and safer. There is no need to read the QR code on the card. It is enough to bring an NFC contactless card to the NFC reader.

Live cards

The customer always has his loyalty card.

Wallet Passes complement or replace your existing plastic cards. Instead of SMS messages, you can now send push messages to customers.

Live card structure.

It is easy to update the design of cards and information on them. Discounts and bonuses are updated on cards in real time from your CRM.

Customer is always near.

Customer store your card in his phone. As soon as the customer is near you, the card will remind about you with a push message.
